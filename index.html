✦ Hello, I'm

Aurelia

I'm an HCI researcher and urban designer with one conviction: that technology and the design of our cities should be open to everyone — not just those who can afford them, or already know how to use them.

This space is where I gather experiments like constellations — prototypes, field notes, and tools for agents and interfaces — each one trying to lower the barrier between people and the systems that shape their lives.

Thanks for being here. This is something we get to build together.
